A neighborhood bakery with a personal welcome

Our story

North Star Bakery began with a simple idea: make everyday bread and treats that bring neighbors together. A loaf for dinner, a cookie after school, or a cake for a milestone can all be part of the same welcoming neighborhood routine.

We bake in small batches and listen to what our customers enjoy. Whether you stop in before work or plan a family celebration, we want your visit to feel familiar and unhurried.

How we choose our ingredients

We focus on dependable flour, seasonal fruit, and ingredients chosen for flavor. We seek nearby suppliers when practical and adjust our seasonal selections to what is available.

Small batches help us plan production thoughtfully. If you have questions about an ingredient, ask before placing a request. Our shared kitchen cannot guarantee allergen-free products.

Meet the people behind the counter

Our bread baker

Our bread baker prepares dough, watches each batch, and helps customers find a loaf for sandwiches, toast, or the dinner table.

Our pastry and cake team

The pastry and cake team plans the daily display and works with families and event planners on flavors, portions, and decoration.

Our counter team

Our counter team welcomes commuters and regulars, explains the day's selections, and helps coordinate pickup inquiries.

Let us bake for your next gathering

From a shared breakfast to a neighborhood celebration, tell us what you have in mind and we will discuss suitable options.
